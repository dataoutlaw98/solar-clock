Solar Clock Privacy Policy

Effective November 14, 2026

Solar Clock is a Chrome extension that shows the time on a solar clock, where sunrise is always 6:00 and sunset is always 18:00. To do that it needs to know roughly where you are. This page explains what it does with that information.

The short version

  • Your location is used only to calculate the clock, the sun, the moon and the seasons, and to show your place name.
  • Everything Solar Clock saves stays in your browser, on your device.
  • There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. The developer never receives any of your data, and nothing is sold or shared for any other purpose.
  • Two map services receive the minimum needed to name places and search for them, as described below.

Your location

If you allow it, Solar Clock asks Chrome for your current location. All the clock, sun, moon and season calculations run inside the extension, on your device. Instead of sharing your location, you can search for a place and use that.

What is sent to other services

ServiceWhenWhat it receives
Nominatim (OpenStreetMap Foundation) To show the name of the place at your location Your coordinates and your browser's language
Photon (Komoot) When you search for a place What you type, your browser's language, and the location currently shown (to list nearby matches first)

These requests go straight from your browser to those services, so like any website they also see your IP address. Their own policies apply: OpenStreetMap Foundation privacy policy, Komoot privacy policy. Nothing else is sent anywhere.

What is saved on your device

Solar Clock uses Chrome's extension storage, which stays in your browser profile, to remember:

  • your last known location and its place name, so the clock appears right away next time
  • a place you chose by searching, if you did
  • whether the clock is popped out into its own window, and where that window was

Removing the extension deletes all of this.

Permissions

  • Location (geolocation): to find where you are, so the clock matches your sunrise and sunset.
  • Storage (storage): to save the settings above on your device.

Solar Clock does not read or change the web pages you visit.

Changes
